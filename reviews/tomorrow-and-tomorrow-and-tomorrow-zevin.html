Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow

Gabrielle Zevin

★★★★★ Read August 16, 2022

Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow surpassed all my expectations with the depth of its characters and its beautiful motifs in real and virtual worlds. It's a captivating study of how passions in life and relationships affect each other. Zevin manages to weave nostalgia and tangible emotion into a dazzling story while offering insights into a wide range of social issues.
