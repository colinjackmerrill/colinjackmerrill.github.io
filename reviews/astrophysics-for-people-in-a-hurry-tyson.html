Astrophysics for People in a Hurry

Neil deGrasse Tyson

★★★★☆ Read April 8, 2022

Tyson strikes a good balance between technical explanations and more relatable dialogue, inserting some well-appreciated humor at various points of the book. Plus, once you get past the deluge of scientific facts, there lie a couple of profound observations at the end of the book that go beyond astrophysics and help the reader see the universe from the "cosmic perspective".
