Book of Night

Holly Black

★★☆☆☆ Read September 30, 2022

This was one of those books I knew I had to read because if I didn’t I would always wonder how good it was— the premise sounded too good to ignore. But, it turned out to be like a hastily thrown together dish that was decent enough to keep me eating, but that wasn’t the meal I hoped it would be. Truly, Book Of Night has most (if not all) the ingredients of a great book, but there’s something about the way that it is put together that is just average and “blah”. The poor execution of this story, one that has so much potential, is sure to leave the reader with the hollow feeling of disappointment.
