Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell

Susanna Clarke

★★☆☆☆ Read November 2, 2023

Imaginative, fascinating, deep, beautiful.

Drawn-out, tedious, tangential.

If you want a study in how a novel can be all of those things at once, dive into reading Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell.

Otherwise (and unfortunately), this is a book I probably wouldn’t recommend in good conscience.
