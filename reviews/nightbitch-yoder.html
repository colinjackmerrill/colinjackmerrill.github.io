Nightbitch

Rachel Yoder

★★★★☆ Read November 28, 2024

Whew.

Nightbitch is quite a ride, one that readers won't be likely to forget soon after reading. This roller-coaster of a read takes some time to gain speed, but once it hit its stride it leaves you with some mixture of delight, surprise, exhaustion, and maybe even nausea. It may even stir up some visceral, animalistic instincts.

My advice: Buckle up and open your mind. Nightbitch is an intense trip, one that has the potential to offend, but also to connect you to the sublime.
