My Friends

Hisham Matar

★★★★★ Read April 17, 2025

In My Friends, Matar spins the story of three Libyans exiled in the U.K. into an exemplary case study of the human condition that is strikingly relatable. His beautiful prose makes for a deeply enjoyable and memorable reading experience that will likely have you adding this novel to your "read again" pile.
