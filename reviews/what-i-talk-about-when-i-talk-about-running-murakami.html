What I Talk About When I Talk About Running

Haruki Murakami

★★★★★ Read January 28, 2025

“Pain is inevitable, suffering is optional.”

In this deceptively simple memoir, Murakami reflects on the irrepressible link between his writing life and his passion for running. He writes candidly about preparing for marathons, training alone, and the strange, meditative space you enter while running. The book feels like philosophy in motion in that Murakami reveals that running isn’t just physical training, but a metaphor for endurance, creativity, and the solitary craft of writing.

If you're into running, writing, or just curious about the discipline behind creativity, this memoir offers a quietly powerful portrait of what it takes to keep going, mile after mile, word after word.
