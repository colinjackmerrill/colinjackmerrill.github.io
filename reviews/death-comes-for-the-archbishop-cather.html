Death Comes for the Archbishop

Willa Cather

★★★★☆ Read July 27, 2025

I went into Death Comes for the Archbishop not knowing what to expect—the title felt dramatic, but the story is anything but. It’s a slow-paced, reflective, and quietly emotional novel about a life of service and faith in the American Southwest.

Cather follows Father Jean Marie Latour as he arrives in New Mexico in 1851 to serve as Apostolic Vicar. Over nearly forty years, he navigates a vast, unforgiving landscape shaped by Mexican and Indigenous traditions, spreading his faith gently and persistently.

Rather than focusing on conflict, the novel offers a meditative portrait of a changing region and a man shaped by it. It reminded me of Lonesome Dove in its attention to the frontier, though this story centers more on spirituality than adventure. Thoughtful and beautifully written, it’s a classic worth reading.
