Fahrenheit 451

Ray Bradbury

★★★★★ Read January 5, 2022

Fahrenheit 451 is a fascinating book full of vivid imagery and profound, clever dialogue. This is entertaining social criticism at its best. My only complaint is that the book is far too short-- it ends abruptly and leaves you wishing the novel had only just started.
