Departure[s]

Julian Barnes

★★★★☆ Read February 20, 2026

Reading Departure(s) feels like pulling up a chair with the Booker Prize–winning author for one last quiet conversation.

In this book, Barnes leans less on conventional storytelling and more on a blend of memoir, essay, and reflection. He meditates on happiness, love, aging, and the art of writing itself, offering readers a window into the mind of one of contemporary literature’s sharpest observers.

Barnes’ personality shines throughout. He's witty, reflective, and unflinchingly honest. While the book lacks the narrative tension or plot-driven momentum of his novels, his voice remains engaging, making the experience quietly satisfying.

For fans of Barnes, this is a rare opportunity to read his last words of wisdom and reflection. I’m giving it 3.75/5, largely for the insight and charm of his voice.
