The Neon Bible

John Kennedy Toole

★★★★★ Read August 26, 2025

4.5 ⭐

John Kennedy Toole once dismissed The Neon Bible as a “bad book.” I have to strongly disagree. Yes, it's not perfect and he wrote it at sixteen, but what struck me is just how good it actually is—an engrossing, atmospheric, and surprisingly mature exploration of small-town life in the American South.

Through the voice of young David, Toole creates a vivid, realistic portrait of growing up under the weight of poverty, religion, and societal judgment. David’s narration feels raw, authentic, and deeply human. At times it is tender and at other times heartbreaking.

What’s most fascinating is how much promise this novel shows. You can see in these pages the early sparks of the literary genius who would later give us A Confederacy of Dunces.

This is an absolute must-read for fans of Toole or of Southern Gothic literature in general.
