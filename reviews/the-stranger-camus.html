The Stranger

Albert Camus

★★★★★ Read January 11, 2022

My review of this book? "It is not important".

I hate that I love this book, and I love that I hate it. "And so?"

This is just another story about an average man, the things that happen to him, and the things he thinks and does (or doesn't do).

Whether or not you like the man or his story, "the world will go on as before" you read it.

I suggest: open yourself up to the "benign indifference of the world" of Camus.
