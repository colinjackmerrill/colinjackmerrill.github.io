The Safekeep

Yael van der Wouden

★★☆☆☆ Read July 24, 2025

Having now read The Safekeep, I now wish it had been safely kept away from my TBR.

When characters start doing things that are unexpected, it signals to the reader to be patient, it will all make sense. In my opinion, that doesn’t happen in The Safekeep. While the plot may make sense in theory, I feel that the characters motivations didn’t.
