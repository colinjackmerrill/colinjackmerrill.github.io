Perfection

Vincenzo Latronico

★★☆☆☆ Read December 1, 2025

Vincenzo Latronico name-drops Animal Collective, vaporwave, and Helvetica Neue like he’s speed-running a Hipster Starter Pack. Perfection is 120 documentary-style pages (or a crisp 3-hour listen) of hipsters in their natural habitats: obsessing over aesthetics, curating their spaces, and quietly imploding under the weight of self-awareness. The concept may seem clever, but the total absence of dialogue or emotional heat leaves the whole thing feeling more like an art installation than a story.
