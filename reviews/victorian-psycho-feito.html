Victorian Psycho

Virginia Feito

★★★☆☆ Read August 7, 2025


Victorian Psycho is entertaining, weird and disturbingly humorous. The novel’s violence is simultaneously unsettling and fun.

The novelty wore off a bit around the 75% mark, but it’s still an easy, fast-paced read that horror fans or lovers of dark humor will likely enjoy.

Not a new favorite, but I’m glad I read it!
