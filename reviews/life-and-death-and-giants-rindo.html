Life, and Death, and Giants

Ron Rindo

★★★★☆ Read September 28, 2025

Thank you to St. Martin's Press for providing this book for review via NetGalley.

Life, and Death, and Giants is a novel about faith and community that follows Gabriel Fisher, an extraordinary young man whose unusual size and talents deeply impact his family, neighbors, and strangers whose lives he touches.

The writing is smooth and accessible, with a diverse cast and multiple perspectives that add richness to the story. Seeing Gabriel through different lenses gave the novel emotional depth, and I especially appreciated the glimpse into Amish life.

Some plot points, however, felt a little unbelievable, and the focus on those around Gabriel left me wishing for a deeper look at his inner life. The story also leaned predictable at times, though Rindo’s storytelling kept me turning the pages.

Overall, this is an engaging read that many fans of general fiction will enjoy. While not groundbreaking, it offers warmth, readability, and a moving exploration of family and community. For me, it landed at 3.75 stars (rounded to 4/5).
