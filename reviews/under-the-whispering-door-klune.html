Under the Whispering Door

T.J. Klune

★★★☆☆ Read December 26, 2021

3.5 stars. Under The Whispering Door is an entertaining mix of humor and existentialism, with a touch of romance. While the premise of the novel is quite simple, Klune weaves several interesting concepts into the story that make it an enjoyable read.
