In Our Likeness

Bryan VanDyke

★★★★☆ Read August 13, 2024

3.75.

In Our Likeness finds the sweet spot in between science fiction and contemporary fiction. Many readers may be drawn to VanDyke's novel because of the buzz-topic "A.I.", but they will keep reading for the mystery and depth that he weaves into the story. In Our Likeness is far from perfect, but the few problems the book has are easy to overlook because they don't prevent it from being a fun read.
