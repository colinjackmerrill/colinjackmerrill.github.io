A Prayer for the Crown-Shy

Becky Chambers

★★★★★ Read July 13, 2022

Becky Chambers continues this stunning series with another warm hug of a book that really makes us ponder several things in life many of us may take for granted. Our reunion with our amazing heroes is joyful, but just like the first entry in the series, this too-short book flies by, and before we know it we are once again saying farewell to Sibling Dex and Mosscap. However, as sad as the parting is, it is also glorious, as their wonderful dose of philosophy and wisdom has fed our hearts and our minds, reminded us to be more intentional in life, and left us that much further along on our path to enlightenment.
