Wise Blood

Flannery O'Connor

★★★★☆ Read February 14, 2026

Wise Blood follows Hazel Motes, a war veteran who returns to the American South determined to reject belief and start a “Church Without Christ,” only to become entangled with a cast of unforgettable misfits. It’s a cornerstone of Southern Gothic—grotesque, darkly comic, and deeply concerned with questions of belief, morality, and the human urge to find (or flee) meaning.

O’Connor’s genius is on full display here. I had to pick my jaw up off the floor more than once at how vividly and convincingly she brings these characters to life; they feel exaggerated and startlingly real at the same time. The novel is strange in the best way, fearless in its themes and unflinching in its vision. I instantly understood why this book is considered both a classic and a defining work of the Southern Gothic tradition.
